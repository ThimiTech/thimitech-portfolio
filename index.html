About Us

Thimi Tech is a Nepal-based software company, delivering intelligent digital solutions for businesses and governmental bodies. We specialize in development of geoportals, municipal management systems, AI and machine learning applications, data visualization, and modern web development. Our mission is to empower organizations with innovative, scalable, and purpose-driven technology that enhances governance, streamlines operations, and drives meaningful impact across communities.

Our Mission

We are committed to creating impactful digital solutions that transform businesses and enhance user experiences. Through innovation and expertise, we deliver excellence in every project.

Smart Governance

Empowering governance and businesses with innovative smart technology solutions that streamline operations, enhance citizen engagement, and drive digital transformation. We develop comprehensive platforms that enable data-driven decision-making, automate administrative processes, and create transparent, efficient systems for public and private sectors.

Geospatial Innovation

Building cutting-edge geoportals and municipal tools for enhanced spatial intelligence. Our geospatial solutions transform complex geographic data into intuitive, interactive visualizations that help organizations make informed decisions about land use, infrastructure planning, resource management, and urban development.

AI-Driven Solutions

Leveraging AI/ML technologies for smarter, data-driven decision-making. We create intelligent systems that automate complex processes, predict trends, and provide actionable insights. From custom chatbots with trainable knowledge bases to predictive analytics platforms, our AI solutions help businesses optimize operations and deliver exceptional user experiences.

Data Intelligence

Transforming complex data into clear, actionable business insights. We design powerful data management systems and interactive dashboards that turn raw information into strategic advantages. Our visualization tools enable real-time monitoring, comprehensive reporting, and data-driven decision-making across all levels of your organization.
